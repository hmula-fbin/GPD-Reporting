About the numbers

The same rules the scorecard uses

NPD pipeline

Projects in Refresh & Sustain, Grow the Core and Create & Transform. Valued at annualized net sales.

CI track

Continuous-improvement projects, valued at annualized contribution margin (savings). Kept out of the NPD total.

In scope

Every project in the data. The scorecard opens on In Progress projects in the Active Phase; use the Phase and Project Status filters to widen it. CRQ (compliance / regulatory) is reported on its own.

Freshness

Figures are live every time a page opens. Use Refresh now on the scorecard for the very latest; it keeps one snapshot per month for the trend.
